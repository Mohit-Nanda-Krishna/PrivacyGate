6. The three lines model
The Group applies the three lines model to establish unambiguous accountability. The model is a statement of accountability, not of
organisational structure: an individual's line is determined by the activity performed, not by reporting line alone.

6.1 First line of defence
The first line comprises revenue-generating businesses and the operational and support functions that execute transactions and
processes. It owns its risks outright, designs and operates controls, performs RCSA, self-identifies issues, records incidents and
remains accountable for remediation to completion. Accountability is not transferred by the existence of second-line oversight or third-
line assurance.
6.2 Second line of defence
The second line comprises the Group Risk function, Compliance and the Data Privacy Office. It sets the framework, taxonomy,
methodology and policy; defines appetite metrics and tolerances; provides independent, evidenced challenge to first-line assessments;
aggregates and reports the Group risk profile; and approves exceptions and acceptances within delegated authority. Where a second-
line function operates a control of its own — for example sanctions screening, operated by Fumiko L. Nakagawa's team — it is a first-
line activity for that control and is subject to independent challenge accordingly.
6.3 Third line of defence
Group Internal Audit provides independent assurance to the Board on governance, risk management and internal control. It has
unrestricted access to all records, systems, personnel and premises. It does not design controls, make management decisions or own
risk. The Chief Internal Auditor, Ifeoma C. Adeyemi (EMP-41455, i.adeyemi@cadencefg.com), reports functionally to the Chair of the
Audit Committee and administratively to the Chief Executive; her appointment, removal and remuneration are matters reserved to the
Audit Committee.
6.4 Independence safeguards
SAFEGUARD REQUIREMENT
Reporting lines The Group CRO (M. T. Achebe) reports to the Chief Executive and has a direct, unfettered reporting line to the Chair
of the Board Risk Committee (A. P. Brightwell).
Appointment and removal Appointment or removal of the Group CRO, Chief Compliance Officer or Chief Internal Auditor requires approval of
the relevant board committee.
Remuneration Second and third line remuneration is not linked to the financial performance of the businesses they oversee.
Budget Second and third line budgets are approved by the relevant board committee and cannot be reduced by business
management.
Right of veto The Group CRO may veto any transaction, product or initiative assessed as outside appetite, subject only to Board
Risk Committee override.
Access Second and third line functions hold private sessions with their board committee at least twice a year without
management present.
6.5 Fourth-line assurance
External auditors, regulators and independent specialist reviewers provide a further layer of assurance. Their findings are recorded in
the Group Issue Management System on the same basis as internally raised issues, subject to the same tracking, escalation and
closure verification requirements set out in Section 15. Current external assurance contacts are Sunita R. Balakrishnan (Halloran & Pike
LLP, s.balakrishnan@halloranpike.example, +1 917 555-0111) and Karsten Ohlendorf (Northwind Analytics GmbH,
